# (0.16.53) MIFM1's screen mirrored over USB (fm1-base's device/mirror.c with patches/fm1-base-mirror-tiles.patch;
# the site's mirror page decodes the same stream): the stream each session sends, and the screen at its end, which
# tools/mirror_check.py compares pixel by pixel (make mirror-check; the CI after the screens).
#   mirror_still  a viewer asking at power-on, the screen still: everything in a few redraws
#   mirror_slow   a link at 3 bytes a millisecond (a sixteenth of USB's) while it plays and pages change: frames
#                 dropped, then at USB's pace, stopped: the screen whole again (the knobs' 60-pixel tiles too)
#   mirror_tour   at USB's pace, playing: FX and its faces, a module, STEP mode, the patterns (SEQ held), GLO, its
#                 second face; stopped
#   mirror_lfo    an LFO's page (its scope moving: drawn when the ring has room, after the rest), then HOME, still
